You describe what you want, and it orchestrates 30+ AI models (including GPT-5, Claude, and Gemini) to deliver finished outputs. Not conversations. Not suggestions. Actual deliverables like presentations, spreadsheets, videos, and yes, even real phone calls. The platform now includes: AI Chat: Multi-model conversations with “Mixture of Agents” that combines responses from ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini AI Slides: Generate presentation decks from prompts in under 60 seconds AI Sheets: Research and data analysis with spreadsheet output AI Docs: Document creation and editing AI Call For Me: Real phone calls made by AI (the feature that went viral in Japan) Sparkpages: Comprehensive research pages with sources and interactive copilot AI Browser: A dedicated browser with built-in Super Agent and ad blocking Genspark’s all-in-one AI workspace consolidates research, creation, and action into a single platform REALITY CHECK Marketing Claims: “One prompt, job done. Complex tasks often need multiple prompts and manual tweaking. The AI Call feature works about 83% of the time based on independent testing. Sparkpages are genuinely useful, but credit consumption is higher than expected. Verdict: Impressive execution on the vision, but set realistic expectations. It’s a powerful assistant, not a replacement for human judgment. Getting Started: Your First 10 Minutes Getting started with Genspark is refreshingly simple. No credit card required for the free plan. Step 1: Head to genspark.ai and click “Get Started.” Sign up with email or Google. Step 2: You land on a clean dashboard with a search bar and example Sparkpages. No overwhelming onboarding, which is nice but might leave newcomers guessing. Step 3: Try your first search. I typed “Best AI tools for small business marketing in 2025.” The Sparkpage loaded in seconds. Instead of blue links, I got a structured summary with sections for “Content Creation,” “Social Media Automation,” and “Customer Engagement.” Each section had explanations, bullet points, and source links. The AI even included recent information from July 2025 articles. Step 4: Test the Super Agent. I asked it to “Plan a 5-day Tokyo itinerary with budget, hotel options, and weather forecast.” The result was impressive: day-by-day breakdowns, hotel categories (budget/mid-range/luxury) with prices, and an embedded weather widget. Multiple AI agents clearly worked together because different sections had different “voices.” Step 5: Try AI Slides. I prompted: “Create a pitch deck for a sustainable fashion startup.” 60 seconds later, I had a 10-slide presentation with cover, problem/solution, market size, business model, and team slides. The designs were decent, not award-winning, but definitely presentation-ready with minor tweaks. A Sparkpage organizes web research into structured, sourced summaries you can actually use The Super Agent: How It Really Works The Super Agent is what sets Genspark apart from ChatGPT or Perplexity . It’s not just answering questions. It’s executing tasks. The Mixture of Agents Architecture Instead of relying on one AI model, Genspark coordinates 9+ specialized LLMs and 80+ tools. When you ask a question: The Super Agent analyzes your request It breaks the task into subtasks Different specialized agents handle each part (research, extraction, reasoning, media generation) A “reflection” step compares outputs and picks the best You get a unified, polished result AI Call For Me: The Viral Feature This feature went viral in Japan, where users asked the AI to make resignation calls to their employers. Here’s how it actually works: You describe what you need (book a restaurant, check business hours, reschedule a delivery). The AI uses OpenAI’s Realtime API to make an actual phone call. A “shadow model” monitors the conversation, handling hold music, ambiguous responses, and transfers. Independent testing showed an 83% success rate across 47 phone calls. The failures mostly came from complex automated menus or calls requiring human judgment. REALITY CHECK Marketing Claims: “Super Agent handles real-world tasks completely autonomously.” Actual Experience: The Call For Me feature works for simple tasks (reservations, inquiries) but struggles with complex negotiations or situations requiring nuance. The 83% success rate means 1 in 6 calls need human followup. Also, not all regions support this feature. Verdict: Genuinely innovative, but treat it as a time-saver, not a replacement for human calls on important matters. Multi-Model Fact-Checking This is where Genspark shines for research. When you ask a question, multiple AIs work in parallel, compare answers, and flag discrepancies. It’s slower than a single model but dramatically reduces hallucinations for research and planning work. Features That Actually Matter (Genspark AI Review) AI Slides Generate presentation decks from text prompts. The system creates structure, adds speaker notes, and generates visuals. You can edit with drag-and-drop or ask the AI to rewrite sections. The built-in fact-check button is genuinely useful. Click it and Genspark identifies which claims have strong sources and which need verification. For client presentations, this saves hours of manual checking. Export to PowerPoint, PDF, or share as a web link. In practice, expect to get 80-90% there, then polish in PowerPoint. AI Sheets This is where Genspark acts like a research analyst. Ask for “top 20 YouTube videos on AI automation” and it pulls views, likes, comments, duration, and tags into a live table. You can also analyze competitor products, compile market research, or build comparison databases. The data extraction is impressive, though you’ll want to verify critical numbers. Sparkpages The original feature that put Genspark on the map. Sparkpages synthesize web results into comprehensive, sourced pages. Unlike Google’s AI Overviews, you get a full document with sections, citations, and an embedded chatbot for follow-up questions. AI Secretary Connect Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, and Notion. The agent can summarize unread mail, pull out action items, draft replies, and suggest meeting times. It’s like having a virtual assistant who actually reads your email. AI Browser Launched in June 2025, this dedicated browser includes the Super Agent, ad blocking, and support for 700+ tools. It can run multiple AI tools in parallel for faster multi-step workflows. A: Yes, Genspark offers a free plan with 100-200 credits per day, unlimited basic searches, and limited Super Agent access. No credit card required. However, serious users typically need the Plus plan ($25/month) for adequate credits and full feature access. Q: How does Genspark AI compare to ChatGPT? A: Genspark focuses on finished deliverables (presentations, research reports, phone calls) using multiple AI models. ChatGPT is better for general conversation, coding, and creative writing. Choose Genspark for research and content creation; choose ChatGPT for versatility and coding. Q: Can Genspark AI really make phone calls? A: Yes, the AI Call For Me feature makes real phone calls using AI-generated voices. Independent testing shows an 83% success rate for simple tasks like restaurant reservations. Complex negotiations or nuanced conversations have lower success rates. Q: What is a Sparkpage? A: Sparkpages are Genspark’s signature feature: AI-generated research pages that synthesize information from multiple web sources into organized, sourced summaries. They include sections, citations, and an embedded AI chatbot for follow-up questions. Q: How fast do Genspark credits get used up? A: Credit consumption varies by task. Basic searches use few credits, but video generation, complex slide decks, and phone calls consume credits rapidly. Many users report the 10,000 monthly Plus plan credits lasting 2-3 weeks with heavy use. Monitor your usage carefully. Q: Is Genspark AI safe and secure? A: Genspark claims GDPR-level compliance and offers a SOC2-compliant enterprise edition. However, independent security analyses have raised concerns about data practices, and official security documentation remains limited. Enterprise users should conduct due diligence. Q: What AI models does Genspark use? A: Genspark orchestrates 30+ AI models including GPT-5, Claude Sonnet/Opus, Gemini, and various open-source alternatives. The Mixture of Agents system selects the best model for each subtask and combines outputs for optimal results. Q: Can I cancel my Genspark subscription easily? A: Multiple user reviews report difficulty finding cancellation options and slow support response times. If you’re concerned, consider emailing support directly with a cancellation request or using your payment provider’s subscription management. Keep written records of all communications. Verdict: Legit Time-Saver for Researchers Genspark is the best “do-it-for-me” agent currently available for research and reports. If you need finished slide decks or deep market research, the $25/month Plus Plan pays for itself in one project. But for coding or simple chats, stick with Claude or ChatGPT. Final Verdict Genspark AI represents a genuine evolution in AI assistants, moving from conversation to execution. The $1.25B valuation and rapid growth aren’t just hype. The platform delivers real value for users who need finished outputs fast. The Mixture of Agents approach, Sparkpages research format, and ability to take real-world actions (phone calls, bookings) set it apart from ChatGPT and Perplexity.
