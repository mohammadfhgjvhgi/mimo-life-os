Press enter or click to view image in full size ▪ Project Type: Evaluatory ▪ Duration: 2 days ▪ Role: UX Design, UI Design, Prototyping ▪ Design Process: Empathize — Define — Evaluate — Design — Test ▪ Tools used: Figma, FigJam, Google Forms About ChatGPT ChatGPT is a large language model created by OpenAI that is based on the GPT-3.5 architecture. It was launched on November 30, 2022 with an aim to understand and generate natural language text, making it possible for people to interact with computers and machines in a more intuitive and conversational manner. ChatGPT has been trained on a massive corpus of text data from a variety of sources, allowing it to generate high-quality responses to a wide range of prompts and questions. ChatGPT is intended to be a powerful tool for a variety of applications, such as customer service, education, and research. Problem Statement The current web interface of ChatGPT lacks intuitive navigation and fails to provide users with an easy way to generate and share results. This leads to a suboptimal user experience. Since ChatGPT is a machine learning model, it is crucial for people to interact with it more so that it can receive data and improve its algorithm. This redesign project aims to improve user engagement, increase ease of use, and enhance user retention by creating a more user-friendly interface with new features that enable users to generate and share results with ease. It introduced me to various approaches of empathizing and understanding the user’s behaviour through primary and secondary research methods like user survey, heuristic evaluation and more. The most challenging part was sticking to a particular task flow while solving the usability issues. Trying to solve all problems is not always feasible! My most valuable lesson has been to approach design problems one at a time, in a well-reasoned way that provides a better experience to the users and impact the business positively. For further scope, I would like to see the feasibility of the new feature upgrades in this case study and improve on them to provide an even better and quicker response to ChatGPT users. I would also like to explore more on the UI part and experiment with micro interactions to make the interface more inviting.
