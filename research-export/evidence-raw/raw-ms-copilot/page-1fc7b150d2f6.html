AI prompting 101: How to write the best AI prompts

The information you can find and the things you can create with artificial intelligence (AI) are limitless when you harness the AI capabilities of Copilot . Whether you’re a seasoned AI enthusiast or just dipping your toes to try it, find out how easy it is to use these tools when you give Copilot detailed AI prompts. Learn the ins and outs of effective AI prompt writing so you can write the best AI prompts the first time you try.

What is an AI prompt?

Simply put, an AI prompt is a command, question, request, or statement that is used to direct an AI tool like Copilot to generate the response, text, or image you want. The way you frame the AI prompt determines the quality and relevance of the results you receive. Once you’ve mastered the art of AI prompting writing, the sky’s the limit.

How to write the best AI prompts

Writing the best AI prompts requires a combination of creativity, precision, and clarity about results you want. Whether you want to use AI as a free image generator , grammar assistant , or something else, here are some tips and tricks to help you excel with your AI prompting:

1. Be clear and specific

Outstanding outputs start with clear AI prompts. Follow these tips for staying specific:

  • Ask for the format you want from Copilot , whether it's a paragraph, step-by-step instructions, or a list.

  • Avoid generic time references; instead, use specific dates or years to enhance precision.

  • Mention specific quantities or criteria when applicable.

  • Incorporate any constraints or preferences to narrow down the AI's response.

  • Make your prompt goal-oriented and practical.

  • Clearly define the scope of your request to guide the AI's response.

  • Always proofread your prompt to ensure it is well-structured and free from ambiguity.

2. Give context

For even greater clarity, include the bigger picture in your AI prompt. Context guides the AI tool to create what you most need based on the wider-view details. Follow these tips for writing well-contextualized AI prompts:

  • Include relevant context, such as the reason for the request or what you plan to do with the output, to help Copilot understand what you need.

  • Clearly outline the task or information you need from Copilot and say how it fits into any larger projects or goals in progress.

  • Ask for the type of response you want, whether that’s guidance, instructions, recommendations, or explanations.

  • Ensure your prompt conveys your intent and what you expect Copilot to deliver.

3. Experiment

To write the best AI prompts, don’t be afraid to play and experiment. You’ll learn what works well for your purposes and maybe even discover Copilot uses you’d never considered. Follow these tips on experimenting with AI prompts:

  • Expand your thinking on what Copilot can do: it isn’t only helpful in finding new recipes or forms of entertainment, but it’s also useful in planning vacations , getting personalized advice, translating foreign text, generating AI pictures , and more. Experiment with Copilot by finding new ways to use it throughout your day at home , work , and beyond.

    • Example: "I need ideas for a romantic date night at home. Suggest creative activities, dinner recipes, and a romantic playlist."

  • Tailor your prompts to various scenarios in your daily life.

    • Example: "I'm working on a presentation about renewable energy. Can you help me gather recent statistics and compelling facts on solar power?"

  • Seek personalized advice or recommendations for your specific needs.

    • Example: "I have a job interview tomorrow. Provide tips on dressing professionally and handling common interview questions."

  • Request assistance with tasks like translation or calculations.

    • Example: "Translate this English text into Spanish: 'Hello, how can I help you today?'"

  • Experiment with different output formats for your information needs.

    • Example: "Can you provide a list of the top 10 sci-fi novels of all time, along with their average reader ratings in a downloadable spreadsheet?"

  • Continuously explore new ways to use Copilot in your daily routines.

    • Example: "I'm hosting a virtual trivia night. Help me come up with a set of challenging trivia questions on famous landmarks."

  • Be creative and open-minded in your requests.

    • Example: "Share interesting historical facts about ancient civilizations, focusing on their architectural achievements."

  • Challenge Copilot with diverse tasks to discover its capabilities.

    • Example: "Find inspirational quotes from famous philosophers and match each quote with a relevant AI image for a motivational poster."

4. Use keywords

Keywords help Copilot understand your preferences and deliver more accurate results. They also help narrow down your search and get the information you need faster. Try these tips to use keywords that work in your AI prompts:

  • Incorporate relevant keywords or phrases to clarify your intent in the AI prompt.

  • In professional contexts, use industry-specific terms to refine your AI prompts.

  • When discussing technology or software, use technical terms relevant to your task.

  • Inquire about products or services with keywords that identify your specific requirements.

Try these AI prompt examples

Want to see these AI prompting tips in action? Here are a few example AI prompts that apply specificity, context, experimentation, and/or keywords. Use these yourself or alter them to ask Copilot for what you need.

Travel planning prompt

Help me plan a trip to Chicago from [location] for next summer. I need flight, lodging, transportation, and activity information. Provide a response that mixes text, tables, and images to give me the best info and fullest picture of what my trip can be. Use these keywords: best Chicago activities for kids, cheap airfare, and Chicago budget travel. Also, tell me three more ways you can help with this trip.

Productivity prompt

I want to save time on daily tasks at work. How can you help me do each of these tasks more quickly without losing the human touch: read and respond to emails, write proposals, manage three employees. Ask me any questions that would help you understand my specific needs, and then give me actionable guidance. Act as a productivity coach.

Study plan prompt

I need help learning high school calculus. Create a study plan that includes videos, a glossary, diagrams, and practice tests to help me master the foundational concepts. Also, keep my interest by making all examples practical. Provide additional resources related to calculus simplified, calculus in real life, and calculus help.

Once you understand AI prompting and know how to write an AI prompt that works well,, a world of possibility opens to you. By applying these tips to write detailed, well-structured prompts—from image prompts to spreadsheet prompts—you can harness the power of AI to receive accurate and relevant results fast.
