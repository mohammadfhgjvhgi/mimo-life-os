Automatically organize your inbox

Respond faster to what matters most

With Auto Labels, Superhuman AI will automatically separate marketing, cold pitches, and social network updates, so you can respond faster to what matters most. You can even make your own Auto Labels with short AI prompts like "job applications" or "requests to review work".

Eliminate clutter for good

Superhuman AI can automatically archive marketing, cold pitches, and social network updates. Turn on Auto Archive, and eliminate inbox clutter for good.

Stay focused and in flow

You can make your own Auto Labels with short AI prompts, and then split your inbox into tabs using these custom Auto Labels. So even when your inbox is overflowing, you can focus on a specific topic — and get through emails of the same type, at the same time.

Emails that write themselves

Turn an idea into an email

Write faster than ever before. Just jot down some phrases, and we'll turn them into a full email. Best of all, the email sounds like you. Superhuman AI matches the voice and tone in the emails you've already sent, applying that to everything it creates.

Follow up on time, every time

If your email needs a response, we'll automatically remind you to follow up. We'll even write the email for you, so all you need to do is hit send. You'll never drop the ball again.

Reply twice as fast

Imagine waking up to an inbox where every email has a draft reply. You would simply edit, then send. Sometimes, you wouldn't even edit. With Instant Reply, our users consistently write emails twice as fast.

Write polished emails with your voice

Tap and speak. Write with Voice turns your thoughts into polished emails that sound like you, ready to review and send in seconds. More than transcription, it understands your intent and drafts complete messages right from mobile.

Work made effortless

Stop searching, start asking

Get work done without having to remember senders, guess keywords, or scan subject lines. Just ask "where's the offsite" or "reply with 3 coffee spots near the office." Ask AI will find what you need — across your inbox, calendar, and the web. What took minutes now takes seconds.

Schedule with just a tap

With just one tap, create an event, complete with title, description, location, attendees — as well as the best date and time — all from your email. And if you're scheduling with your team? We'll find a great time when you're all free.

Auto Summarize conversations

See a 1-line summary above every conversation. As new emails arrive, it updates instantly. And if you want to see more detail, just hit expand. You'll see the 1-line summary first, you can often skip reading the email. You'll fly through your inbox.
