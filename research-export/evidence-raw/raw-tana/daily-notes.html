Questions and answers

Can I open the day node of another workspace by default?

It is not possible to change which workspace's day node opens by default. It is fixed to your private workspace's day node.

However, you can open the sidebar and hold Option (Mac) or Alt (Windows) while clicking on a workspace to open its day node for today.

How can I get the task list on my daily notes back?

You may have seen a Tana Outliner setup that had a task list on the daily notes page, or had one from our early prototypes that has since disappeared. There is a simple way to get it back.

Go to any daily note and make a search that looks for:

  • all #task nodes
  • NOT DONE

Run it to confirm it is fetching the right things. Adjust how you want your tasks to look (set Display, Group, and Sort options here). Confirm changes to the view options and the query, then right-click the title of the search and select Move to Related content.

How can I set up Unsplash banners on my daily notes?

You can plug in your own Unsplash API key to get access to their images for the banners of your daily notes page. Ambassador Andre has a walkthrough of the setup on X.

This is an experimental feature and may change in the future.
