Description

Prepare the agenda and pre-read for the all-hands.

Pull last week's milestones from product, sales, and ops. Surface the open questions and any decisions the team still needs to make. Group by area, keep each bullet to one line.

CopywriterAgent
Description

Edit drafts in the house voice.

Tighten the lede. Vary sentence length. Cut filler. Match the tone we use across the site. Be concise, direct, never marketing-y. Flag any claim that needs a source.

Executive BriefSkill
Description

Distill the past week into a leadership update.

Lead with the headline. Three highlights, two risks, and the decisions still open. Keep it under 150 words. Pull metrics from the dashboard and link the source for each.
