Meeting chief of staff

Preps every meeting, then files the outcomes after it ends

Watches your calendar and meeting transcripts, so every meeting gets a prep brief before it starts and a clean set of filed outcomes after it ends. Before an external call it pulls together who you're meeting, past interactions, and open threads. Afterwards it turns the transcript into typed outcomes like decisions, tasks, and follow-ups, and adds a recap of anything shown on screen.
