FAQ

What if Tana isn't right for me?
You get a 30 day free trial with no credit card required. And if you sign up to the yearly plan you also get a 60 day refund window to make sure you're happy.
What about my data?
Your data is yours. You will have access to it even if you downgrade or cancel your plan. You will be able to export it to other systems.
What happens if I run out of AI credits?
You can top up AI credits with custom amounts whenever needed.
What does Tana replace?
Tana can replace your meeting platform, company wiki, docs and project management, AI notetakers, and a large portion of your AI assistants. You have access to the latest Claude, Gemini, and OpenAI models, but with a solid context graph that helps you get more from them.
Can I use Tana with other tools?

Yes. Tana has many integrations so you can connect with tools like HubSpot, Jira, Linear, and GitHub. It also has MCP, so you can set up connections to almost any type of tool.

You can also use the botless meeting agent to capture context from meetings hosted in Zoom, Teams, or Google Meet.
