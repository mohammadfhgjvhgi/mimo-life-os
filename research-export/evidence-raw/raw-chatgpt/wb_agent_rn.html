Introducing ChatGPT agent

ChatGPT agent allows ChatGPT to complete complex online tasks on your behalf. It seamlessly switches between reasoning and action—conducting in-depth research across public websites, uploaded files, and connected third-party sources (like email and document repositories), and performing actions such as filling out forms and editing spreadsheets—all while keeping you in control.

Learn more in the ChatGPT agent FAQ.

ChatGPT agent is available now for users on Pro, Plus, and Team plans.

With ChatGPT agent’s built-in virtual browser, the core functionality of Operator has been integrated. The standalone Operator experience at operator.chatgpt.com will be deprecated in the coming weeks.
