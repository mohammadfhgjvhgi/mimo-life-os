# Plans and Pricing

> Choose the Craft subscription plan that best fits your needs – from individual users to large teams.

Craft offers subscription plans tailored to meet the needs of individual users, families, teams, and businesses. Whether you're a solo creator or a large organization, there's a plan designed for you.

## Plans Overview

### Starter Plan (Free)

* **Content**: Up to 1,500 content blocks
* **Storage**: 1 GB
* **Media Upload**: 25 MB per file
* **Version History**: 7 days
* **AI Assistant**: Usage quota (15 credits)
* **Ideal for**: Individuals getting started with Craft for personal projects

### Plus Plan

* **Pricing**: \$96 annually or \$10 monthly
* **Access**: Unlimited documents, up to 5 spaces
* **Storage**: 1TB per space
* **File Upload Limit**: 5GB per file
* **Version History**: 30 days
* **AI Assistant**: Monthly usage quota (50 credits)
* **Features**: Custom domains, advanced share analytics
* **Ideal for**: Solo professionals and creators looking for extended capabilities

### Family Plan

* **Pricing**: \$180 annually or \$18 monthly
* **Members**: Invite up to 5 members
* **Shared Spaces**: Up to 5 shared family spaces for collaboration
* **Features**: All Plus plan benefits for each family member
* **Ideal for**: Families or small groups aiming to collaborate together

Each invited family member gets their own account upgraded to Plus, including **5 personal spaces** with 1TB storage each – separate from the shared family spaces.

<Tip title="Creating shared spaces">
  Shared Family spaces are created from **Team Settings**, not from "My Spaces." Go to **Settings > Team > Spaces** to create a new shared space that all family members can access.
</Tip>

### Team Plan

* **Pricing**: \$600 annually or \$60 monthly
* **Members**: Invite up to 10 members
* **Shared Spaces**: Up to 25 shared team spaces for collaboration
* **Features**: All Plus plan benefits for each team member
* **Ideal for**: Small to medium teams requiring collaborative spaces

Each invited team member gets their own account upgraded to Plus, including **5 personal spaces** with 1TB storage each – separate from the shared team spaces.

<Info title="Need more than 10 seats?">
  [Contact us](mailto:support@craft.do) for custom pricing options for larger teams.
</Info>

Visit the [Craft pricing page](https://www.craft.do/pricing) to view our latest pricing.

## View or Change Your Subscription

You can access and modify your current plan details:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Go to **Settings**
  </Step>

  <Step>
    Select your **Team**
  </Step>

  <Step>
    View **Plan Overview**
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pro Tier Legacy

<Info title="Note">
  If you subscribed to the Pro Tier in the past, your subscription will be kept at Pro Tier, but you can upgrade anytime.
</Info>
