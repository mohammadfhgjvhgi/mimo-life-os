Use Gems in Gemini Apps
You can create and save Gems to customize responses in Gemini Apps to your specific needs.

What you need

To create and use Gems with a personal Google Account, you must also be 13 (or the applicable age in your country ) or over.

To use this feature, you must be signed in to Gemini Apps. Learn how to sign in to Gemini Apps .

Important: When you create or chat with a Gem, remember that you agreed to Google's Terms of Service , including the Prohibited Use Policy . Be sure not to violate others' rights. Use your discretion before you rely on, publish, or use content that Gemini Apps generate. Users with a work or school account may be subject to different terms. Learn more about how to use Gemini Apps with your work or school Google Account .

Learn how to get started with Gems 

Use the Gems guide to get ideas for how to use Gems, learn how to get a quick start with premade Gems, and find suggestions for writing great Gem instructions.

Get started 

Create, use & manage Gems

Important :

For now, Gems can’t be used with Gemini Live.

The Learning coach premade Gem doesn’t currently support language learning.

Create a Gem

Important: When you create a Gem in the Gemini web app, it will appear in the Gemini mobile app and the Gemini side panel in Google Workspace.

On your computer, go to gemini.google.com .

Click Open Sidebar .

Click Gems .

If you’ve never made a Gem before, first click Settings and help Gems . 

Click New Gem .

Enter a name and instructions for your Gem.

(Optional) To add sources, under "Knowledge," click Add files .

Click Save .

Other optional steps to improve your Gem

Use Gemini to re-write your instructions 

After a few words, you can click Use Gemini to re-write instructions to have Gemini Apps create more comprehensive instructions for most topics. It may take a few seconds for the generated instructions to appear.

Upload files to give your Gem more context 

You can add files to give your Gem more context or to reference specific docs in your chats with the Gem. Learn more about supported file types and limits .

After you add instructions, you can upload files. Under "Knowledge," click Add files .

To upload a file from your device: click Upload files .

To add a file from your Google Drive: click Add from Drive .

To add files from your Drive, you need to have Keep Activity on and connect Google Workspace to Gemini Apps. If it’s not connected, you'll get the option to connect it. Learn about your Gemini Apps and Google Workspace data .

If you add a file from your Drive, Gemini will use the most recent version of the file. If you make changes to the file, these changes will be reflected in your Gem.

To add a NotebookLM notebook: click More uploads Notebooks .

Tips: 

If you don’t want the Gem’s responses to include citations to your knowledge files, select Disable knowledge citations . This also disables citations for files uploaded in your Gem chats.

You may need to scroll down to find the "Knowledge" section.

You can edit a Gem to remove a file or add more files later. Learn how to edit your Gem. 

Preview your Gem 

In the text box on the right, enter a prompt to preview the Gem and make edits to the instructions as needed.

Click Submit .

Use a Gem in a chat

On your computer, go to gemini.google.com .

Click Open Sidebar .

Click Gems .

If you’ve never made a Gem before, first click Settings and help Gems . 

Under "My Gems," select the Gem you want to use.

In the text box at the bottom, enter your question or prompt.

Click Submit .

Edit a Gem

Go to gemini.google.com .

Click Open Sidebar .

Click Gems .

Under “My Gems,” click the Gem that you want to edit.

Make edits.

Click Submit .

Delete a Gem

Go to gemini.google.com .

Click Open Sidebar .

Click Gems .

Under “My Gems,” find the Gem that you want to delete.

Click Options Delete .

Share a Gem or a conversation with a Gem

You can share Gems from Gemini Apps or a conversation you had using a Gem.

Learn how to share a Gem .

Learn how to share a conversation .

Important: Gems that are shared with you aren't automatically added to your Gem manager. Once you open the Gem and interact with it, it will be added under "My Gems".

Try experimental Gems

Experimental Gems allow you to try out early-stage Gemini features through Gems and share feedback. Experimental Gems are in development, and their quality and availability may vary. You can leave feedback to help us improve the overall experience.

Why Gemini Apps might remove a Gem

Gemini Apps may remove Gems when our systems detect a possible violation of Google's Terms of Service , including the Prohibited Use Policy .

Users with a school account or a work account that has Gemini Apps as a core service are subject to different terms. Learn more about how to use Gemini on your work or school Google Account .
