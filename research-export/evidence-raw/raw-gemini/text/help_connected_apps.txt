Use & manage Connected Apps in Gemini
Gemini Apps can connect to other apps to complete your request and give you more helpful responses. With your permission, Gemini can also help you with your info and content in other apps.

Here are a few examples of what you can ask Gemini to help you with:

Boost productivity: Summarize emails from Gmail, create events in Google Calendar or third-party apps, or help with code from GitHub.

Find & play media: Play music from YouTube Music or Spotify, or search your Google Photos or Samsung Gallery.

Connect with people: Make calls and send messages with your default apps or WhatsApp on Android devices.

Control your devices: Manage your smart home devices in Google Home or use the Utilities app on Android devices to set alarms, change device settings, and more.

Learn more about apps Gemini can connect to .

Tip: You can also get personalized Gemini experiences based on your data in certain Google apps when you connect them to Gemini Apps. Learn more about personalization based on Connected Apps .

Important for users with a work or school account: The info below is for users who access Gemini Apps with a personal account. If you’re using a work or school account, learn how to use Connected Apps in Gemini with a work and school Google Account .

Get started with Connected Apps in Gemini

Important: For now, Gemini can’t use Connected Apps in Gemini in Google Messages.

Sign in to the Gemini app. You must be signed in to use Connected Apps. Learn how to sign in to Gemini Apps .

Check your Keep Activity setting. 

When Keep Activity is off, Connected Apps won’t be available on gemini.google.com , iOS devices, or smart watches. On Android devices, only the Utilities, Phone, Messages, and WhatsApp apps will be available if the setting is off.

Choose which apps Gemini connects to. You can connect or disconnect apps in your Connected Apps settings anytime. Learn how to connect or disconnect apps in Gemini .

Gemini automatically uses public information from certain Google services. Learn more about Gemini and these Google services .

How to use Connected Apps in Gemini

Important: Some Connected Apps are designed to automatically work with Gemini. You can disconnect apps in your Connected Apps settings anytime. Learn how to disconnect apps in Gemini .

On your computer, go to gemini.google.com .

In the text box at the bottom, enter your question or prompt.

To specify an app for Gemini to use, enter @ and select the app. If the app you select isn't connected, it’ll either be connected or Gemini will ask for your permission to connect it.

Click Submit . If an app is available, Gemini automatically uses it.

Follow any on-screen instructions.

Browse available apps

You can find apps that you can connect to Gemini in your Connected Apps settings. Learn more about the apps on the Connected Apps settings page .

On your computer, go to gemini.google.com .

At the bottom, click Settings & help Connected Apps .

If you don’t find “Connected Apps,” first click Personal Intelligence Connected Apps . Learn about Personal Intelligence and Connected Apps .

Browse Connected Apps 

About apps in Connected Apps settings 

The apps in Connected Apps settings vary by Gemini app, device, country, and more. For example, apps that are only available in the Gemini mobile app on Android won’t show in Connected Apps settings on iOS devices or in the Gemini web app at gemini.google.com .

Also, Gemini automatically uses public information from certain Google services. Connected Apps settings don’t change Gemini’s use of this public information. Learn more about Google services that Gemini automatically uses info from .

What happens when you disconnect an app in Gemini 

How your data is handled when you use Connected Apps in Gemini 

You can disconnect or connect apps in your Connected Apps settings anytime.

On your computer, go to gemini.google.com .

At the bottom, click Settings & help Connected Apps .

If you don’t find “Connected Apps,” first click Personal Intelligence Connected Apps . Learn about Personal Intelligence and Connected Apps .

Find the app you want to connect or disconnect.

To connect an app, turn it on .

To disconnect an app, turn it off .

Follow any on-screen instructions.

About Google services that Gemini automatically uses info from 

Gemini automatically uses public information from certain Google services, including:

Google Search

Google Flights 

Google Hotels 

Google Maps 

YouTube 

Gemini won’t access your personal information or content in these services without your permission.

To use public information from Google Flights, Google Hotels, Google Maps, and YouTube, Keep Activity must be on. Gemini can always use public information from Google Search, even if Keep Activity is off or you’re signed out.

Connect custom apps for Gemini Spark

Important: For now, custom Connected Apps are only available to users with Gemini Spark access and within Spark tasks. Learn more about Gemini Spark .

You can connect personal or third-party apps by linking their Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. This adds a custom app in your Connected Apps settings. Once connected, you can use the custom app in your Gemini Spark tasks to build highly customized workflows. Learn how to connect custom apps .

Learn more about Connected Apps

Important: Availability of Connected Apps vary by location, language, device, and Gemini app. For details, review the articles below.

You can learn about the tools and services that Gemini Apps can interact with below.

Tip: Some Connected Apps are available in your Gemini Live chats. Learn how to use Connected Apps in Live chats .

Communication apps 

On Android phones and tablets, you can ask the Gemini mobile app to make calls and send messages with:

Your default calling app with the Phone app 

Your default messaging app with the Messages app 

WhatsApp 

Tip: You can use Gemini to make calls or send messages with these apps even when Keep Activity is off. Learn how to connect and disconnect these apps .

You can also ask Gemini Apps to find and manage details about people in your contacts with the Contacts app .

Device control apps 

You can ask some Gemini Apps to control your:

Smart home devices with the Google Home app 

Android mobile device with the Utilities app , including actions like:

Set & manage alarms & timers in your clock app

Open websites, apps, and settings

Check and control device features

Tip: Gemini can use the Utilities app even when Keep Activity is off. Learn how to connect and disconnect this app .

Lifestyle apps 

You can ask Gemini Apps to help with everyday tasks, like exploring apps, grocery shopping, or booking reservations, using the following Connected Apps:

Google Play : Find and install apps from the Google Play Store.

Instacart : Shop for groceries and household items.

OpenTable : Find, book, and manage reservations.

Zillow : Find rental properties, review tour schedules, and book tours.

Media apps 

You can ask Gemini Apps to play media and more from:

Spotify 

YouTube Music 

You can also ask Gemini Apps to search for photos, videos, and more from:

Google Photos 

Samsung Gallery 

Productivity apps 

You can ask Gemini Apps to interact with a number of apps and services through the Google Workspace app , including:

Gmail 

Google Calendar 

Google Docs 

Google Drive 

Google Keep 

Google Tasks 

On specific Android mobile devices, you can ask the Gemini mobile app to interact with third-party productivity apps, including:

HONOR Calendar 

HONOR Notes 

Lenovo Notes 

OnePlus Mind Space 

OnePlus Notes 

OPPO Calendar 

OPPO Notes 

Samsung Calendar 

Samsung Notes 

Samsung Reminder 

TECNO Calendar 

TECNO Notepad 

vivo Notes 

Xiaomi Calendar 

Xiaomi Notes 

You can also connect and use the following apps:

Dropbox : Find, summarize, share, and manage your Dropbox files and folders.

GitHub : Import a GitHub repository to your chat and ask Gemini about its code.

Google Business Profile : Manage your Business Profile on Google and create a Business Notebook with proactive insights.

Travel & activities apps 

You can ask Gemini Apps to help you with your travel plans. Gemini can find activities and experiences that match your preferences using Connected Apps, like:

Viator : Find activities and experiences for your next trip.
