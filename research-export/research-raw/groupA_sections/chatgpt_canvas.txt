By far, my most commonly used LLM AI tool, as much as I really enjoy the benefits/capabilities from Claude, Gemini, and many, many others. ChatGPT is the only LLM I currently pay for due to some of the features OpenAI helped pioneer. For me personally, it’s my favorite Projects + Canvas workflow that ships half‑baked ideas into finished assets. My goal in this article is to provide an example of how I use these tools on an everyday basis. Let’s begin with this as an example for us to follow: Spin Up a Project Press enter or click to view image in full size The first step is to create a new Project. Let’s call it: Community Pulse — April Issue. Inside a Project, you get a full creative workbench: Saved chats: Whenever you dive into an idea deeper (like “what are good headlines?”), The chat stays connected to the Project, so you don’t lose the conversation. This allows you to organize your thoughts on a common subject or personal project without having to scroll through a list of chats. Like having a contact in your text app vs random numbers with no context. Base Data-File saving built-in: When you generate something useful — a chart, an HTML/Python file, a data table — you can immediately save it inside the Project itself. No downloading to your desktop, no wondering where you put it. It’s pinned right there under ‘Files’ inside your Project, organized and ready whenever you come back. Whether it’s the final newsletter HTML, a pie chart from Discord stats, or a mockup image for the header, everything you create lives in the same clean workspace. Tailored Projects: I honestly don’t use this function as often, as I feel it is more for GPT’s, but if you are looking to use a project as a persona or have it remember the reason why you are using the project, you “Add Instructions” to allow the Project to stay focused on your task and answer accordingly without having to prompt it in every chat. Some of this wording may be unfamiliar to you, but hang with me, and I will post some recent videos under each of these 2 sections for you to be able to follow along from OpenAI: Open a Canvas Inside Your Project Press enter or click to view image in full size Once your Project is spun up, now comes the star of the show: Canvas . Normally, when you’re brainstorming with ChatGPT in a single chat thread, things get lost: You scroll back, you forget which version was better, you lose track of how you got there. Canvas fixes that by making every thought visible and movable. Similar to how you would work with someone in a live document, like Google Docs-but with AI. And because it’s built into Projects, you can keep multiple Canvases tied to the same overall idea without clogging up your chat history or desktop files. Subscribe Subscribe Remember me for faster sign in Inside a Canvas, you get a wide-open space to: Write and prompt Drag and drop text blocks Write code alongside AI Expand individual thoughts into drafts Highlight sections and ask ChatGPT to improve or expand them Instead of losing track of your flow in a giant wall of text, Canvas lets you see the structure of what you’re working on. You can map out a whole newsletter, a product launch plan, a blog series — whatever you need — without it feeling overwhelming. Key Takeaway: Treat the Canvas Like a Whiteboard Don’t be afraid to make mistakes. Post the messy draft, circle weaknesses in plain text, and tell GPT to rebuild just that section. Speed beats perfection, and Canvas keeps every revision one scroll away. The 20‑Minute Jump‑Start Open a fresh Project — name the outcome, not the task. Create a Canvas inside it; paste any raw notes/ideas you might have Provide a guiding prompt — explain what you want to put in the Canvas on your existing highlighted text or in the main chat. Ask for a v2 (“Turn these bullets into a LinkedIn post”). Inline iterate — highlight weak phrases, prompt replacements. Export or schedule the finished asset in your preferred format, and before you know it: you are onto the next idea. Five Plays to Steal Workshop in a Box Draft an agenda in a Project, open a Canvas pane, and ask GPT‑4o to flesh out slides one section at a time. You iterate bullet points while it paints icons — no PowerPoint thrash. Code Together, Literally Create Pygame/HTML logic inside Canvas, on your own or with chat. Press Run inside of ChatGPT to see it work and highlight portions of the code to learn and debug. Rapid Content Calendar Map a month’s worth of posts in a Canvas. For each, ask GPT to suggest hashtags, draft captions, or rewrite headlines based on target audiences — you can then align them based on how soon you want to kick one off. Data‑Rich Sales Sheet Upload the Excel export, ask Python to chart trends, embed the plot, then let GPT draft the persuasive copy underneath. Living SOPs Each procedure lives as a Canvas doc; when the process changes, you prompt‑edit inline and export the updated PDF — no hunting old versions. Closing Ultimately, I believe you will find yourself understanding how to use Projects and Canvas tools as soon as you do one thing: START Fire a thought into Canvas, massage the idea alongside AI, then organize your thoughts in Projects. Or the other way around. That’s the workflow lighting up my Medium drafts, client decks, and now (hopefully) you will consider doing so as well. *I am not paid by ChatGPT to write this.
